# Managing Multiple Godowns Effectively

*How to stop stock-outs and track inventory across multiple warehouses without manual counting.*

## 1. Introduction

The logistics of moving truckloads of feed between transit godowns (warehouses) and retail shops can be a nightmare. Without real-time tracking, it is easy to lose track of where specific batches of inventory are located.

## 2. The Danger of Stock Mismatches

Manual counting and paper registers inevitably lead to stock mismatches. When a truck unloads 500 bags at Godown A, but the register is kept at the main shop, delays in communication result in lost inventory, expired stock, and tied-up capital.

## 3. Live Digital Tracking

Utilizing offline-first software like ScaleERP allows you to track transfers instantly. You can create a digital "Stock Transfer" entry that deducts inventory from the main godown and adds it to the retail shop's inventory, ensuring an accurate count at all locations.

## 4. End-of-Day Audits

Best practices dictate reconciling digital records with physical counts. ScaleERP provides an End-of-Day Audit report that highlights exactly what should be on the floor, allowing your managers to spot discrepancies immediately rather than waiting for a monthly audit.
