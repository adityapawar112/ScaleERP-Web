# Automating Payment Recovery via WhatsApp

*Stop making awkward phone calls. Use WhatsApp automation to collect pending dues faster.*

## 1. The Cash Flow Problem

In the wholesale distribution and agricultural retail business, cash flow is king. However, a significant portion of capital is often tied up in pending party dues. Tracking these ledgers manually leads to delayed payments and stalled inventory purchasing.

## 2. The Awkwardness of Phone Calls

Calling 50 different parties daily to remind them of their outstanding balances is not just manual labor—it creates friction and awkwardness between you and your long-term customers.

## 3. The Power of WhatsApp

WhatsApp has a 98% open rate in India, vastly outperforming SMS or email. A professional, automated WhatsApp message serves as a polite but firm reminder, providing clarity on the exact amount owed without the confrontation of a phone call.

## 4. Implementing Automation

ScaleERP features built-in WhatsApp automation. With two clicks, you can select multiple overdue ledgers and blast formatted payment reminders instantly. The system auto-fills the party's name, the outstanding amount, and the date, saving you hours of manual typing and follow-ups.
